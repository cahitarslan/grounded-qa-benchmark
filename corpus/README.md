Format: first line `# <title>`, then `<!-- id: <code> -->`, then one `## Madde N - <heading>` per article.
Repealed articles ("Mülga") are kept out. Added articles keep their official number, e.g. `## Madde 73/A`.
Transitional articles ("Geçici Madde N") are kept as `## Madde Geçici/N`. Amendment notes such as `(Değişik:24/3/2022-7392/4 md.)` are kept as published; footnote markers and the footnotes themselves are dropped.
